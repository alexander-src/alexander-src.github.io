# DESIGN.md: `alexander-src.github.io`

## Design Read
Reading this as: interactive 3D research portfolio and systems logbook for hiring managers and senior engineers, directed by the user's AXON visual reference (`studio.motionsites.ai/explore/axon`), dial **ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3**.

## Visual Identity and Rationale (R-31)
- **Visual Direction (R-37):** Explicit user direction targeting a full-viewport dark-void 3D particle sculpture behind a minimal spatial research-logbook interface, inspired by AXON's luminous point-cloud aesthetic without generic SaaS templates, glow blobs, or marketing buzzwords.
- **3D Centerpiece (`assets/webgl-field.js`):** Zero-dependency custom WebGL2 / GLSL 300 es particle engine rendering 52,000 procedural point sprites with real-time cursor turbulence, orbital parallax, depth bokeh, and smooth GPU morphing across four mathematical sculptures (Toroidal Ribbon Manifold, x86-64 Instruction Gyroscope, Dual Verification Helix, and Hyperbolic Relinker Mesh) triggered by project focus or category filtering.
- **Motion Control (R-19, R-39):** Explicit `PAUSE 3D` / `PLAY 3D` button in the top instrument bar pauses or resumes continuous 3D motion at any time, and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` automatically renders a calm static 3D frame.
- **Palette and Contrast (R-01, R-21, R-25, R-29):** Void black ground (`#030306`), obsidian surface scrim (`#0C0D12`), crisp ice-white ink (`#F2F3F7`, 18.58:1), secondary silver (`#C2C6D6`, 12.11:1), muted slate (`#9DA3B8`, 7.73:1), and one restrained cool-lavender technical marker accent (`#B9A6FF`, 9.77:1) used strictly for bracketed logbook headers (`[INFO]`, `[AIMS]`, `[EXPLORING]`, `[CONNECT]`) and active focus indicators. Dark default is justified directly by the optical luminance requirements of a light-emitting 3D particle cloud; the long-form case study supports reading comfortably in dark or light mode, and `cv.html` ships on crisp print-ready white paper.
- **Typography (R-06):** Compact uppercase technical monospace (`ui-monospace`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Menlo`, `Consolas`) for spatial instrument chrome and logbook headers matching the AXON research-terminal reference, paired with clean system sans-serif for readable long-form dossier and case-study body prose.
- **Composition and Rhythm (R-05, R-14):** Spatial left-rail study gallery + center 3D sculptural void + right-rail floating research logbook on desktop, transitioning cleanly to a vertical scroll layout with full >=44px tap targets on tablet and mobile (R-03). Zero hero-plus-4-stat-cards, zero fake terminal chrome, zero 3-step AI cards, zero pill eyebrows.
- **Copy Discipline (R-02, R-15, R-16, R-17, R-36):** Zero em dashes anywhere across the entire repository, zero fabricated metrics, and specific action labels on every control.
