isa-audit

Rust, v0.2.0 (2026)

Static x86-64 ELF64 instruction auditor written in safe Rust with zero dependencies. It scans executable ELF sections, decodes legacy, REX, VEX, and EVEX instructions, diffs builds for ISA regressions (isa-audit diff --fail-on-regression), and fails CI with exact addresses and symbol offsets when forbidden instructions slip into a binary.

427 KB stripped binary. Decodes /usr/bin/objdump at 281.6 MiB/s (~70M instructions/sec) single-threaded with 0 recovery steps, verified against live g++ compilations across x86-64 ISA flags (SSE, AVX, FMA3, AVX2, BMI, AES, SHA-NI, VAES, VPCLMULQDQ).
